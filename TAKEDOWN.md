# Takedown policy

**Contact:** firstloveprovisional@proton.me

## What we will do

If you hold rights in a work catalogued here, or in material we reference, and you want something
removed or corrected, **tell us and we will act — without argument or delay.**

Specifically, on request from a rightsholder or their representative we will:

- remove any cover image reference
- remove any outbound link
- correct or remove any field
- remove an entire record

We will not ask you to justify the request, and we will not require a formal notice. A plain email
is enough.

## What this project already avoids

This may resolve most concerns before they arise:

- **No manga is hosted or reproduced.** No page images, no chapter content, no mirrors.
- **No cover image files are stored.** Covers are referenced only from publisher-supplied reuse
  feeds, on the publisher's own servers, and stop appearing the moment the publisher withdraws
  them.
- **No publisher synopsis, review or blurb text is stored.** What we hold are bibliographic facts —
  title, creators, publisher, dates, ISBN, serialisation — together with our own notes.
- **Links point only to authorised sources** — publishers, official platforms, legitimate
  retailers. Never to scanlation or piracy hosts.
- **Works marketed or intended as pornography are excluded** and hold no record.

## Records and removal

This database is designed as an archive: records are normally *marked* rather than deleted when a
source stops carrying a work, so that the historical fact of publication survives the commercial
availability of the work.

**That principle does not apply to rightsholder requests.** It exists to resist churn in
third-party bibliographic databases, not to resist you. A removal request results in removal, and
the record does not return.

## Errors and corrections

If something here is simply wrong — a misattributed creator, a bad date, a misclassification —
please tell us. Corrections are welcome from anyone, rightsholder or not, and can also be raised as
an issue or a pull request.
