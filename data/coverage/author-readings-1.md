# Author readings, batch 1

Sixty pen names checked against outside sources. **42 found, 18 not found.**

Nearly all of the confirmed readings come from the National Diet Library's OpenSearch
records, which carry a `dcndl:creatorTranscription` alongside `dc:creator`. Where the NDL
record identifier begins `R100000002` the cataloguing is the library's own; where it begins
`R100000137` the record comes from the publisher's registration feed, so the reading is the
publisher's.

Readings are given in katakana, with a space between the family and given parts of a two-part
name. NDL prints its transcriptions with a comma at that boundary; the comma has been replaced
by a space and nothing else has been altered.

The machine guess column reproduces the value currently stored, for comparison. Twenty of the
42 confirmed readings differ from it.

## What was searched

The National Diet Library OpenSearch endpoint was the main instrument: a `creator=` query for
every one of the 60 names, then a `title=` query on the example work for each name that came
back empty or ambiguous. Where a creator query matched, the record's other titles were read
back to check that the artist is the one we hold rather than a namesake.

For the names NDL does not know, the publisher platforms were fetched directly: Shonen Jump+,
Comic Days, Tonari no Young Jump, MAGCOMI, Sunday Webry, 一迅プラス, キミコミ, Comic Action,
Big Comics, 花ゆめAi, and the ComicWalker work API. Of these only MAGCOMI publishes an author
profile block, which is how 森 and ぽん太 were resolved. The GigaViewer sites expose an internal
author id but no author page (`/author/<id>` returns 404).

manba.co.jp refused every request with a Cloudflare 403 and was not pursued further. Web search
returned nothing usable for any of the unresolved names; its Japanese index does not appear to
reach pen names at this level of obscurity.

## Caveats worth carrying forward

- **三鼓詩** is the one row where the source and the machine agree on the sounds but disagree on
  the spelling, because NDL normalises づ to ズ. Neither form is confirmed as the artist's own.
- **星埜かなた** is sourced from a book that is not one of ours; only the pen name ties the two.
- **ぽん太** rests on the artist's X handle, not on a printed reading.
- The 18 not-found names have no published book between them, which is the whole reason nothing
  was locatable. If any of them later gets a tankobon, an NDL creator query will resolve it.
