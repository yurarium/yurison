# Author readings, batch 3

Kana readings for the 60 pen names in `authors-3.json`. The `reading` field in that file is a
morphological guess and was treated as a starting point only, never as evidence.

**Found 35 of 60. Not found 25.**

Every reading below comes from a National Diet Library record, reached through
`https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/api/opensearch` with a `creator`, `title` or `any` query, and read off
the `dcndl:creatorTranscription` field. One reading, 南瓜かぷちー, is corroborated by 版元ドットコム,
which carries the same publisher-supplied kana. Where a record splits the name with a comma, the
split below follows it; where the record writes the kana unsplit but the name has a plain kanji
boundary, the note says so.

The 25 unresolved names are mostly web-only work: one-shots and contest entries that never reached a
volume, so no catalogue holds them. Showing no furigana for these is the right outcome.

## Method and what was refused

Requests carried the User-Agent `yurarium/0.1 (bibliographic database; +https://yurarium.github.io/)`
with roughly 1.5 seconds between calls to a host. Nothing refused the crawler outright, but several
routes turned out to carry no reading at all and were abandoned:

- ComicWalker (KADOKAWA) stores no kana on its author or title pages. Its author index is sorted by
  reading, which is suggestive but not a stated reading.
- The GigaViewer episode JSON used by となりのヤングジャンプ, コミックDAYS, 少年ジャンプ+ and
  ichicomi.com carries a series title and no author reading.
- BOOK☆WALKER, ブックライブ and コミックシーモア show the author name without kana.
- 版元ドットコム has readings but only for the publishers that feed it, which covers few of these titles.
- Ordinary web search is thin on these long-tail Japanese pen names and produced no usable source.
