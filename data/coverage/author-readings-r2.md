# Author readings, round 2

79 names checked. 39 found, 40 not found.

Of the 39 found, 13 contradict the machine guess on the kana themselves and 26 confirm it, several
of those after moving a word boundary. The largest corrections are 苹果知生, guessed ヒョウカ トモオ
and actually メロコ チキ; 美鈴ちょこ, guessed ミスズ and actually ミレイ; 朱子すず, guessed シュシ
and actually アケコ; 鷹条色s, whose trailing `s` is read カラーズ rather than left as a letter; and
毒田ぺパ子, guessed ドクデンペパコ and actually ドクタ ペパコ, which the artist's own X handle
`@pepperco` confirms as a Dr Pepper pun.

Nearly every found reading comes from the National Diet Library, which records
`dcndl:creatorTranscription` beside `dc:creator` on each book it holds. Where NDL marked the
family/given boundary with a comma it is given below as a single space. Where NDL wrote the
transcription unbroken and the name is a single unit or a set phrase, the reading is reproduced
unbroken and the note says so.

The 40 not-found names split into two groups. Most have no catalogued book at all: their credits
are web chapters, one-shots on a publisher's free platform, or contest entries, so no cataloguer
has ever written the reading down. A smaller group (出水, 史織, 鎌田, 春都, 純玲, 仲村春) is made of
short common-kanji names where NDL holds hundreds of namesakes and none of them is this artist;
those were paged to 200 records each before being given up on.

x.com returned HTTP 402 to every unauthenticated request, so artist X profiles could be reached
only where a publisher page quoted the handle. comicspace.jp timed out on every attempt.
