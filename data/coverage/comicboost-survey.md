# comicブースト survey

Every `/content/` page listed in https://comic-boost.com/sitemap.xml, read on 2026-08-04 and
judged against [DEFINITIONS.md](../../docs/DEFINITIONS.md). 161 pages, one request each with a
1.5 second pause, identified as `yurarium/0.1 (bibliographic database; +https://yurarium.github.io/)`.

## What the platform gives us

comicブースト is 幻冬舎コミックス's own web magazine, so its search tags are publisher-side and
count toward `marketing_label` under DEFINITIONS 4. It runs a 百合 tag and applies it to 21 of the
161 pages. That is the strongest single piece of evidence available here, and under the inclusion
test in DEFINITIONS 2 the label alone is enough: `marketing_label ≠ none` puts a work in without
any argument about content.

The site also runs an アダルト tag, on 5 pages. None of those 5 carries the 百合 tag, so the two
sets do not overlap and no work had to be decided on both grounds at once. No page on the host
carries a 成年コミックマーク, an R-18 or 18禁 designation, or an age gate, and the tankōbon this
survey checked (女王のトランク, はなまる・エントロピー) are バーズコミックス, a general imprint.
By the mechanical test in DEFINITIONS 7 the platform therefore publishes nothing that is marketed
as pornography. One work, 猫とシュガーポット, is still held out in its own section below on this
survey's stricter instruction to drop anything borderline on that ground, and 女王のトランク is
excluded partly for the same reason. Both rows say so.

## What the survey could not establish

- The five 百合作品集 posted on 2025-01-31 (おねだりしてみて, 猫とシュガーポット,
  日常やめたらしたいこと, あなたの夜が明けたら, ガールズ イン ザ ヘル) carry no book link on
  their comicブースト pages, and 幻冬舎コミックス's site search returned nothing for their titles.
  Their tankōbon publisher and imprint are therefore unknown from anything read here. Every
  chapter of each shares one date, which is the 出張掲載 signature: their first publication is
  the print collection, not this platform, so under DEFINITIONS 6 they are print candidates that
  this platform led us to rather than web serialisations.
- 百合にはさまる男は死ねばいい！？ is the same shape, seven chapters on one day, and is already
  recorded that way.
- Several excluded works are genuinely uncertain rather than clearly out, and their rows say so.
  漁港の肉子ちゃん and 母の宗教に困っています are both parent-and-daughter works that a literal
  reading of 「close relationship between women, of any kind」 could reach. オタク女子が、４人で
  暮らしてみたら。, ヴァン子さんは無職, 魔王様、投了です, In Between and
  その日、朱音は空を飛んだ each leave something undetermined by the page's own words.
